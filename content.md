# Page: PICC/Central Lines Insertion and Care
**URL:** https://www.atriummedicalcenter.com/long-term-acute-care-hospital-ltach-our-services/picc-or-central-lines-insertion-and-care
**Title:** PICC/Central Lines Insertion and Care | Long-Term Acute Care Hospital (LTACH) in Stafford, TX

Home » Our Services » PICC/Central Lines Insertion and Care

Our team employs the use of Peripherally Inserted  Catheters (PICC lines)/ Central line insertion  as a safe and effective way to provide long-term intravenous (IV) access for patients requiring extended treatments, such as long-term antibiotic therapy or nutritional support. Lines are inserted into a vein in the arm and threaded toward the heart, offering reliable access for medications, fluids, and blood draws over an extended period. Our experienced team of physicians and specially trained nurses perform the PICC/Central lines insertion in a sterile, controlled environment to ensure the procedure is as safe and comfortable as possible for each patient.

# Page: Hyperbaric Oxygen/ HBO
**URL:** https://www.atriummedicalcenter.com/long-term-acute-care-hospital-ltach-our-services/hyperbaric-oxygen-or-hbo
**Title:** Hyperbaric Oxygen/ HBO | Long-Term Acute Care Hospital (LTACH) in Stafford, TX

Home » Our Services » Hyperbaric Oxygen/ HBO

We provide hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBO) as an advanced treatment option for patients with certain chronic conditions and severe wounds that are not healing through traditional methods. During HBO, patients breathe 100% oxygen in a pressurized chamber, which helps promote the healing of tissue by increasing oxygen supply to damaged areas. This therapy is particularly effective for conditions like non-healing diabetic foot ulcers, chronic infections, radiation injuries, and severe burns. Our medical team works closely with each patient to determine if HBO is the right option for their condition and carefully monitors the therapy to ensure safety and optimal results. By integrating hyperbaric oxygen therapy into our comprehensive care plans, we help enhance recovery, reduce the risk of complications, and improve overall outcomes for patients facing challenging health conditions.
